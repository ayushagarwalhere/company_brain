# Engineering compensation bands, 2026

Confidential: engineering leads and HR only.

## Salary bands
- Engineer I: 70,000 to 90,000 a year.
- Engineer II: 90,000 to 120,000 a year.
- Senior Engineer: 120,000 to 155,000 a year.
- Staff Engineer: 155,000 to 190,000 a year.

## Process
Salary reviews happen once a year in March. Bands are reviewed against the market every September.
